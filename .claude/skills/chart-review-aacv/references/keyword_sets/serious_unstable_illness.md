# serious_unstable_illness

Severity and instability language for criterion [18]. The chart must supply the characterization.

## direct_terms

- acute on chronic
- decompensated
- end-stage
- exacerbation
- life-threatening
- serious
- severe
- threat to life or bodily function
- uncontrolled
- unstable

## aliases

- actively ill
- clinically deteriorating
- critically ill
- poorly controlled

## abbreviations

- CHF
- COPD exacerbation
- DKA
- ESRD
- ICU

## behavioral_clues

- admitted for
- goals of care discussion
- hospice referral
- transferred to ICU

## treatment_terms

- chemotherapy
- dialysis
- home oxygen
- intubation
- transplant list

## negation_patterns

- at baseline
- compensated
- no acute distress
- stable
- well controlled

## note_on_use

The protocol defines neither serious nor unstable. A bare diagnosis is not enough - the severity or instability wording must be in the chart text you quote. The protocol's system list excludes the studied condition, so DLB itself does not count.
