# dlb_visual_hallucinations

Recurrent visual hallucinations, second core DLB feature.

## direct_terms

- Charles Bonnet
- illusions
- misperceptions
- sees children or animals
- sees people who are not there
- sees things
- visual hallucination

## aliases

- sees animals
- sees people
- sees things that are not there
- visual misperceptions

## abbreviations

- NPI
- VH

## behavioral_clues

- Charles Bonnet considered
- family reports the patient describes visitors

## treatment_terms

- clozapine
- pimavanserin
- quetiapine

## negation_patterns

- denies hallucinations
- no hallucinations

## note_on_use

People with DLB underreport. A patient-only denial is not enough to refute - look for informant corroboration.
