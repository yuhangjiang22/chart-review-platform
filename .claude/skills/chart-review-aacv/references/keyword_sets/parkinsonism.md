# parkinsonism

Cardinal parkinsonian features, fourth core DLB feature, and the drugs that can cause them.

## direct_terms

- 4-6 Hz tremor
- bradykinesia
- cogwheel rigidity
- cogwheeling
- decreased arm swing
- hypokinesia
- hypomimia
- increased tone
- lead-pipe rigidity
- masked facies
- micrographia
- pill-rolling
- rest tremor
- resting tremor
- rigidity
- shuffling gait
- slowed finger taps
- slowness of movement
- tremor at rest

## aliases

- cogwheeling
- masked facies
- pill-rolling
- reduced arm swing
- slowness of movement

## abbreviations

- DIP
- MDS-UPDRS
- UPDRS

## behavioral_clues

- gait evaluation by PT
- levodopa trial
- movement disorder clinic

## treatment_terms

- aripiprazole
- carbidopa-levodopa
- haloperidol
- lithium
- metoclopramide
- olanzapine
- paliperidone
- prochlorperazine
- risperidone
- tetrabenazine
- valproate

## negation_patterns

- no bradykinesia
- no tremor
- normal gait and speed
- normal tone

## note_on_use

Spontaneous excludes drug-induced. Judge by timing, not by the presence of a drug on the list - quetiapine, clozapine and pimavanserin are used deliberately in DLB.
