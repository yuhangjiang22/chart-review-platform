# dlb_rem_sleep_behavior_disorder

REM sleep behavior disorder, third core DLB feature.

## direct_terms

- RBD
- REM sleep behavior disorder
- REM without atonia
- acting out dreams
- dream enactment
- falls out of bed
- polysomnography
- punching or kicking in sleep

## aliases

- acting out dreams
- dream enactment
- falls out of bed
- punching or kicking in sleep

## abbreviations

- G47.52
- PSG
- RBD

## behavioral_clues

- bed partner reports
- sleep study ordered

## treatment_terms

- clonazepam for RBD
- melatonin for RBD

## negation_patterns

- no dream enactment
- sleeps quietly

## note_on_use

The patient is asleep during the behaviour and cannot observe it - a patient-only denial is unknown. Distinguish from OSA, restless legs and nightmares.
